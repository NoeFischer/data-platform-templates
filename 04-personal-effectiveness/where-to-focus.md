# Where to Focus

> A thinking tool for the start of each week (or when you feel scattered). When you're not the project lead, nobody hands you a perfectly ordered list. You have to look at the project and decide where your time creates the most impact. This takes 10-15 minutes and pairs with the Friday retro — this one points you forward, that one looks back.

## 3. Where do I have unique leverage?

_Not everything you *can* do is worth your time. Focus on where you're disproportionately effective._

| Lens | Answer |
|------|--------|
| What do I know that the team doesn't? | |
| What can I see that they can't? (outsider perspective, cross-project patterns) | |
| What am I willing to say that they won't? (hard truths, pushback, bad news) | |
| Where does my specific experience apply? | |
| Who trusts me enough to act on my input? | |

## 4. The four-box filter

_Plot your potential work areas. Spend most of your time in the top-right._

|--|------------------------------|----------------------|

| **Low impact on project success** | Tempting but don't. Your leverage is wasted here. | **Stop.** This is busywork, even if it feels productive. |

## 7. The gut check

_One final question before you start the week:_

**If I could only do one thing this week, which one would it be?**



_That's your real priority. Protect time for it._
